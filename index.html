Learning progression

  1. Foundation: konsep, arsitektur, model, dan kriptografi.
  2. Mechanism: consensus dan implementasi blockchain sederhana.
  3. Design: solusi blockchain, smart contract, dan DApp.
  4. Enterprise & Security: Ethereum, Hyperledger, security.
  5. Evaluation: scalability, interoperability, dan use cases.
